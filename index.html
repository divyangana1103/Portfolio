I’m a final-year UI/UX design student from Pune, drawn to intuitive and meaningful digital experiences. For me, good design should not only look refined; it should simplify complexity and solve real problems with clarity.

As I grow in the field, I combine research, structured thinking and visual sensitivity to create user-centred solutions that are both functional and thoughtfully crafted.

    How I work — × —

    Good design starts with listening.

    Four things I hold on to, whatever I’m designing.

    Auto layoutHorizontalGap 24Padding 24

    Simplify the complex.

    Untangle messy systems, like a FASTag ops channel run on spreadsheets, into flows people understand at a glance.

    Clarity over clutter.

    Research before pixels.

    Surveys, interviews, card sorts and eye tracking. Real routines reveal more than assumptions ever could.

    Observe first.

    Design for everyone.

    Accessibility is the baseline, not a feature. If it works with a screen reader, it works better for everyone.

    Inclusive by default.

    Earn trust, keep control.

    AI assists, people decide. Transparency and visible updates turn anxiety into confidence.

    Human in the loop.
